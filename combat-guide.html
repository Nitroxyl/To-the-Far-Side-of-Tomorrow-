PLAYER COMBAT REFERENCE · V6

Pokémon Combat.

Official Pokémon damage, Santara's grid-based encounters. This replaces all V5.3 damage dice and offense/defense tier multipliers.

1. Start a turn

  1. Check your Pokémon's current HP, status, held item and active battle mechanic.
  2. Check movement and the range or shape of your chosen move. One grid square is 5 feet.
  3. Choose a target in range. In competitive battles, resolve priority first, then effective Speed.
  4. Use the Damage Calculator to resolve damaging attacks.
  5. Apply any post-hit effects such as recoil, healing, status chance or stat changes separately.

2. Damage and accuracy

Enter the attacker, defender and move in the calculator. Adjust weather, terrain, screens, critical hits and other relevant conditions. The result is a possible HP damage range; it does not automatically subtract HP from the target.

Moves use their real Base Power, Attack or Sp. Atk, Defense or Sp. Def, STAB, type effectiveness and Pokémon level. Old damage dice are no longer used.

Roll/check accuracy and other random effects separately if they aren't explicitly modeled. In multi-target moves, the calculator accounts for cartridge-style spread mechanics when supported.

3. HP and training

Every Pokémon has perfect IVs of 31 and zero EVs. Santara does not use EV training, so there's nothing to track after encounters. Pokémon levels run from 1 to 100. The damage calculator defaults to the neutral Serious Nature; temporary stat stages from moves and abilities still matter.

Maximum HP

floor((2 × Base HP + 31) × Level / 100) + Level + 10

Shedinja remains at 1 HP.

Current HP is saved in your Pokémon record. If you take 12 damage, subtract 12 from current HP; no other dice multiplier is applied.

4. Movement and range

The campaign converts Pokémon Speed to movement squares for field encounters:

Squares = clamp(4 + floor(Base Speed / 30), 4, 12)

Each square is 5 feet. Move targeting follows its metadata (contact, ranged, spread or special shape). Use the GM's encounter grid ruling for line of sight, obstacles, cones and unusual abilities.

D&D level = ceiling(Pokémon level ÷ 5), used for adventure progression, not the damage formula.

5. Gimmicks and special forms

Terastallization, Z-Moves, Mega Evolution and Dynamax are GM-granted unlocks. Mega Evolution also requires an assigned compatible Mega form and its held Mega Stone. The app enforces which transformations your trainer has unlocked.

Some Z-Move, Max Move, signature weather and custom battle effects still need GM adjudication. Do not treat their preliminary damage preview as a complete turn simulator.

6. Move Memory, items and status

Outside combat, use My Pokémon to choose up to four moves your Pokémon has already learned. Learnset compatibility alone does not automatically teach a move. Your GM handles Egg, tutor and special unlocks; TMs are requested through your Trainer Bag.

Track burn, paralysis, poison and other statuses on your Pokémon page. Keep held items and consumed gimmicks up to date after battles.
